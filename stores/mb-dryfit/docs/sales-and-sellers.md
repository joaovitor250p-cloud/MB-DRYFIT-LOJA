# Sellers, WhatsApp and sales reports

The owner manages the shared directory under **Vendedores e WhatsApp**, also reachable from the sales report and POS. Name, Brazilian WhatsApp number, WhatsApp visibility and an optional authorized account email can be edited. Disabling a seller removes that entry from new sale selection and the public chooser; past sales remain. The optional email links future attributed sales to that authorized staff account. It does not itself create or grant a staff login; access remains controlled in **Equipe e acessos**.

The initial eight contacts provided by the owner are initialized outside migrations in one atomic batch. A durable bootstrap marker prevents later reads or deployments from restoring edited/deactivated contacts. Public responses include only contact ID, name and the approved WhatsApp number. Private account emails never appear in the contact chooser. Contact buttons, product inquiries, cart receipts and account order receipts all open the chooser, carrying the complete original message to the chosen seller. No message is sent automatically.

In the POS the owner selects the responsible seller. Sellers can use only their linked directory entry; a staff account without a directory link retains its own account attribution. The authenticated actor remains in `seller_id` for audit and checkout idempotency. Each sale additionally snapshots the credited seller, linked account email and the product name, exact color/size and photo. A directory or product edit cannot rewrite these historical snapshots. A batch ID distinguishes multi-line sales from individual item lines. The database checks the chosen agent is active when a sale commits.

**Todas as vendas / Minhas vendas** provides today, the last seven calendar days including today, the current month, the last twelve months, a month picker and inclusive custom dates. Boundaries and monthly grouping use São Paulo time (UTC−03 for current operating dates). Summary totals aggregate the entire filtered period; the history paginates in pages of 50, independently of the legacy 300-row dashboard feed. Net value subtracts each line's recorded discount, and sale counts deduplicate batch IDs. Seller totals, monthly comparison and exact variation photos share this period. Sellers see only sales they registered or sales whose credited account email snapshot matches their authorized account. Customers cannot access staff reports or manage contacts.

The storefront gym background is an eight-second, approximately 427 KB muted inline video with native looping, a poster fallback and tab/focus recovery. Autoplay restrictions show a play control. Reduced-motion preferences initially pause it; a user can explicitly resume. The WhatsApp button uses the recognizable vector mark and opens a responsive, animated contact dialog. No celebrity campaign section or 3D fitting feature was added.
