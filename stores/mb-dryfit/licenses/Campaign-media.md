## MB animated identity and Fit Studio (2026-11-09)

The hero now uses the gym training clip from Pexels 4745812 listed above as a dark, desaturated, silent 8-second background (mb-gym-motion.mp4 and its poster). Animated MB typography and light effects are original interface graphics. The earlier apparel montage is no longer used in the hero. No athlete endorsement is implied.

Fit Studio uses Three.js 0.160.0 under the MIT license (see ThreeJS.txt). The faceless mannequin and generic clothing are built from geometry in code. They are illustrative, not a scan or a cloth simulation. Height and weight only change the illustration. Size recommendations require a store-provided body measurement range chart plus the shopper’s entered body circumferences. Body inputs are not sent to the server or persisted.
